Straight talk: I haven't built websites for paying clients yet, so I start with small projects. What I do bring is over a year of hands-on SEO on 22+ client sites, so what I build is made to be found, not just to look good.

How I work

The same five steps on every project, so you always know what happens next.

  1. Talk

    You tell me what you need and who the site is for. I ask a few questions and we agree on the goal.

  2. Plan & quote

    I write down the pages, features, time and cost. You approve it before I start.

  3. Build

    I build the site and share a preview link, so you see progress and not just the final result.

  4. Review

    You check it on your phone and laptop, and I make the small changes you ask for.

  5. Launch & support

    I put it online, set up Google Search Console and a sitemap, and stay available for small fixes.

3-month and 6-month plans

For businesses that want a website and help getting it seen. Pick the shorter plan to launch, or the longer one to launch and improve.

3-month plan: Build & launch

Best for a new business or a first website.

  1. MONTH 1

    Plan and build the core

    Goals, pages, content and design. The main pages are built and shared for preview.

  2. MONTH 2

  3. MONTH 3

    Get found and hand over

6-month plan: Build, launch & grow

Best if you want steady improvements after launch.

  1. MONTH 1–3

    Everything in the 3-month plan

    Plan, build, test, launch and get found.

  2. MONTH 4

    Learn from real visitors

    I check analytics and Search Console, and fix what visitors struggle with.

  3. MONTH 5

    Add and improve

    New pages or features, plus a Core Web Vitals and speed improvement pass.

  4. MONTH 6

    Review and plan next

    We look at what changed, and decide the next steps together.

Questions people ask

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages and features. Send your details through the form below and you'll get a clear written quote before any work starts.

Do you also do SEO?

Yes. Every site I build includes SEO basics, and I worked as an SEO Executive on 22+ client websites. I can't promise rankings, but I make sure the site is fast, well structured and ready to be found.

Do you use WordPress or Shopify?

My main builds are hand-coded with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. I have also managed content and products on WordPress and Shopify stores, so I can help with updates there.

What do you need from me to start?

Your business details, text and photos (or I can help shape the text), a logo if you have one, and a few websites you like. A short call or message is enough to begin.

Who pays for the domain and hosting?

The domain and hosting are billed by the provider to you. I help you choose and set them up, and for small sites free hosting such as GitHub Pages is often enough.

What if I want changes after the site is ready?

Small changes after review are part of the project. Bigger new features are quoted separately, so there are no surprises.
